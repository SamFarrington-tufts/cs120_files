Commonly Asked Questions

How far in advance should we book?

We recommend reaching out 4-8 weeks ahead, especially for weekends and peak seasons.

Do you take song requests?

Yes! We can take requests in advance and, when possible, during the event.

What do you need from the venue?

We typically just need a standard power outlet and a small performance area. We'll coordinate setup details with you ahead of time.

Can you learn a special song for our event?

Yes-if you send it early enough, we can usually learn one special request.

How long is setup and breakdown?

Setup usually takes about 45 to 90 minutes depending on the package and space. Breakdown is quick and we keep it low-impact for the venue.
